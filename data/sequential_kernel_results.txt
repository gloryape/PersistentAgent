  This is a DIFFERENT axis of complexity than harmonic enrichment.
  Harmonics increase d by enriching individual-entry statistics.
  Sequential coupling increases d by requiring pair statistics.

  For coupling to k-step neighbors (θᵢ - θᵢ₋ₖ), d grows as:
    k=1: d=8 (cross-moments with predecessor)
    k=2: d~14 (cross-moments with two predecessors)
    k=N: d ∝ N (cross-moments scale with coupling depth)

  Combined with harmonics:
    K harmonics × k-step coupling → d = O(K·k)
    Full coupling to all harmonics and all steps → d = ∞

─── IMPLICATIONS ───
  The complexity hierarchy has TWO independent axes:

  AXIS 1 (Harmonic): How many Fourier modes of phase?
    cos(θ-θᵢ) → d=2, cos(kθ-kθᵢ) for k≤K → d=2K

  AXIS 2 (Sequential): How many consecutive entries are coupled?
    No coupling → d+0, 1-step → d+6, k-step → d+O(k)

  Total: d = 2K + O(k) where K = harmonic depth, k = coupling depth
  For K=∞ or k=∞ (full Fourier × full history): d=∞
